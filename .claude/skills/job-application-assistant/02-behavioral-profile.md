## Strongest Behaviors
- **Self-learning:** Learns new domains and adapts across business, technical and multilingual work.
- **Persistence and consistency:** Follows long-term goals and improves through preparation and feedback.
- **Independent ownership:** Comfortable working independently while contributing to an organization and team.

## How You Work Best
- Clear goals, priorities and expectations, with managers who allow ownership.
- Practical problems, responsibility, measurable outcomes and continuous learning.
- Collaborative but not overly bureaucratic teams, with room for independent work and long-term contribution.

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Unclear direction or excessive control:** Ask for concrete priorities, checkpoints and ownership boundaries; avoid presenting micromanagement as a personal failing.
- **Routine without growth:** Seek process-improvement or customer-facing responsibilities rather than claiming every repetitive task is unacceptable.

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- Ownership and clear deliverables
- Cross-functional practical problem solving, continuous improvement and learning

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- Continuous coding as the whole job, with little connection to business or customers
- Unclear priorities, micromanagement, unnecessary bureaucracy or purely repetitive work

## Management Style Preferences
- Communicate expectations and priorities clearly, then allow autonomy, ownership and constructive feedback.
- Excessive micromanagement or unnecessary bureaucracy is a likely friction point; verify the actual team culture rather than inferring it from a job title.

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Link self-learning, ownership and improvement to a specific verified example relevant to the employer.
- **CV:** Show concrete business, AI evaluation, security research and project outcomes instead of generic personality labels.
- **Interviews:** Use the candidate's own STAR stubs from `07-interview-prep.md` once they supply full details.
- **Don't overstate:** Do not invent formal behavioral scores, management authority or direct customer-success experience.
